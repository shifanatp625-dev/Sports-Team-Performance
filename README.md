#⚽ Sports Team Performance & Fan Engagement Dashboard
An interactive Sports Team Performance & Fan Engagement Dashboard designed to analyze team performance, player statistics, goals, assists, merchandise revenue, and fan engagement metrics.

📊 Dashboard Overview
The dashboard provides a centralized view of key sports performance and business metrics, including:

🏆 Top Players – Highlights the highest-performing players based on performance metrics.

⚽ Total Goals – Tracks overall goals scored.

🤝 Total Assists – Provides insights into player contributions through assists.

🛍️ Merchandise Revenue – Visualizes revenue generated from team merchandise.

👥 Total Fan Reach – Represents the overall reach and engagement of the team's fan base.

📈 Top 10 Players – Compares player performance using a ranked bar chart.

🍩 Worst Performing Players – Identifies players with comparatively lower performance metrics.

📉 Highest Goals Scored Team – Compares goal-scoring performance across teams.

💰 Merchandise Revenue by Team – Displays revenue generated by individual teams.

📱 Fan Engagement Reach – Breaks down engagement across platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X, and mobile apps.

🎯 Goals & Assists by Player Position – Compares goals and assists across different player positions.

This project demonstrates how sports data can be transformed into an interactive dashboard to help users understand player performance, team performance, fan engagement, and commercial revenue in a single view.
